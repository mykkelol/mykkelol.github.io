About Rapid Freight Inc.

Rapid Freight Inc. was established in 1993, filed as a NVOCC (License No. 14981N, SCAC No. RPDF). We are headquartered in Los Angeles, with CFS and sales teams in every main sea/air port in the USA. We have exclusive offices and partners in more than 50 ports/cities across China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Vietnam and the Far East, making a global network of agents throughout the world, ensuring complete logistical support for all international cargo requirements.

Rapid Freight Inc. is more than a freight forwarder; we are a full-service freight logistics company. We completely fulfill our customers' needs, from international transportation and domestic freight to warehousing and marine insurance, with the following specialties:

  • Ocean cargo export / import
  • Air cargo export / import
  • Full delivery services (DDU / DAP / DDP)
  • Domestic rail, trucking, airfreight, door-to-door service
  • Marine insurance agency
  • Export packing and warehousing services

Special cargo: Project, oversize, hazardous, break bulk, liquid cargo and charter vessel. Also metal scraps, plastic scraps and waste paper.

Special area: China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Vietnam, Singapore, the Far East, South & Central America, and from anywhere in the world to China.

Rapid Freight also maintains long-term contract relationships with steam lines, airlines, truckers, rails and other related industries. We are like a customized program designed to fit your profile at competitive rates.
